QuoxProof receipt viewer

Drop a receipt file (<session>.ndjson) below, or paste its lines. Everything runs in your browser: no upload, no account, no backend. The receipts carry their own public keys, so this page can verify the Ed25519 signatures and the hash chain locally. Optionally add a checkpoint (.tip.json) to check the head has not been truncated.
